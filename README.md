# Escrow ET

## What is Escrow ET?

Imagine you're buying something from a stranger online. You don't know them, they don't know you. If you send them money first, what stops them from just disappearing? And if they ship the item first, what stops you from pretending you never got it?

This is one of the biggest problems with online buying and selling in Ethiopia — and Escrow ET is built to solve it.

Escrow ET is a trusted middleman that holds the buyer's money safely until the item is actually delivered — then releases it to the seller.

## How it works

1. **The seller lists the deal.** They create an escrow contract with the item details, the price, and the buyer's phone number.

2. **The buyer pays — but not to the seller.** The money goes into Escrow ET's secure holding. The seller can't touch it yet. The buyer gets a confirmation that their money is safe.

3. **The seller ships the item.** Once they've sent the product, they mark it as dispatched on the app.

4. **The buyer confirms delivery.** When the item arrives, the buyer scans a QR code or enters a PIN to confirm they received it. This is the moment the money gets released to the seller.

5. **Everyone gets what they were promised.** The buyer got their item. The seller got their money. Nobody had to trust a stranger — the system handled it.

## What if something goes wrong?

If the item never arrives, arrives damaged, or there's any disagreement, either side can open a dispute. An admin at Escrow ET will review the situation and decide whether to refund the buyer or release the money to the seller. Nobody loses their money unfairly — there's always a way to resolve it.

## Knowing who you're dealing with — Fayda ID verification

One of the most important things about a platform like this is knowing that the people using it are who they say they are. Anyone can create a fake name or phone number — so Escrow ET uses **Fayda**, Ethiopia's national digital ID system, to verify real identities.

When you sign up, you scan your Fayda ID QR code. The system instantly checks that it's a real, valid Ethiopian national ID and confirms your legal name and other details from it. Once verified, your account is tied to your actual identity — not just a username someone made up.

This matters for everyone:
- **Buyers** know the seller is a real, verified person — not a ghost account
- **Sellers** know the buyer is genuine and accountable
- **The platform** can hold people responsible if they try to cheat the system

## Who is it for?

- **Buyers** who want to shop online without the fear of getting scammed
- **Sellers** who want to ship confidently knowing they'll get paid
- **Merchants and businesses** who want to offer escrow-protected payments to their customers

## Why does Ethiopia need this?

Online trust is still a major barrier to e-commerce in Ethiopia. Most people either know someone who got scammed buying online, or they avoid online shopping altogether because of that fear. Escrow ET removes that fear entirely — you never have to hand over money and hope for the best. The system holds it until you're satisfied.

In short: Escrow ET is like having a trusted friend hold the cash during a deal, who also checked everyone's ID at the door. You only hand it over once everything checks out — and if it doesn't, you get it back.
